# The Federal Graphics Improvement Program

Several design manuals of major USA government instituions were created by top-tier design firms in the 1970s as a result of the Federal Graphics Improvement Program.
The program was launched under the National Endowment for the Arts and spearheaded by Nancy Hanks, the NEA chairman appointed by President Richard Nixon.
The federal policy objective was to use good design to overhaul their visual identities and streamline operations in order to improve efficiency and public communication. 

* [NASA Graphics Standard Manual](https://archive.org/details/NASA_Graphics_Standards_Manual) 1976 created by Danne & Blackburn <!-- https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/nasa-graphics-standards-manual/ -->
* EPA Graphic Standards System 1977 designed by Chermayeff & Geismar with Steff Geissbuhler
* [New York City Transit Authority (NYCTA) Graphics Standards Manual](https://archive.org/details/nycta-gs-manual/) 1970 designed by Massimo Vignelli and Bob Noorda
